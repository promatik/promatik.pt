This was the very beginning of my developer career, I was in middle school when I started to type some instructions on my TI 84 calculator. At that time I didn't even know I was programming and it was mostly trial and error.

Later on I started to understand more and more, and I began coding simple scripts to solve the new topics we covered each day in math class. Like the formula for second-degree equations, or trigonometric formulas to work out angles and triangle side lengths.

It was, above all, a lot of fun, the thrill of feeling I could harness the calculator's computational power to produce the data I needed.
