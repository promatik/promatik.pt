I started to code on a TI 84 calculator back in 2005, at the time I had no idea that one day I would create a whole 3D engine on it.

This was one of the most challenging things I've done in my life. The TI 84's graphics are basic: a 96×64 pixel screen, 24 KB of RAM and 480 KB of ROM, not to mention the language, Basic.

With these specs, I was able to create an engine to, first, create 3D models, draw them and apply operations like scale and rotation, and even change the perspective of the camera.

I spent countless hours, mostly while going back home on weekends after university.
