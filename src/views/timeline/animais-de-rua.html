Animais de Rua is a non-profit organization that rescues animals off the streets. They were in need of a platform to manage every process related to animal collection and treatment. I was glad I could help, I created their back office from scratch using Laravel, it's all open source on github.com/animais-de-rua, it's a huge project with tons of entities and relations between them and a lot of different permissions for users.

Later on I ended up also creating their website, which is fed with data from the back office. The website is also a webapp, since I had the time, I created everything from scratch with no frameworks, just Vanilla JS, it's just 14K of JS + CSS, everything is cached and shown instantly to the user. Its Google Lighthouse score is near 100%, I tried to apply the best principles and the most recent web methodologies.

I've been collaborating with them since then, there are always some fixes and adjustments to make the platform better.
